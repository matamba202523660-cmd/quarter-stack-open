F.J Garden

F.J Garden is an agricultural business that plants seeds, grows crops, harvests fresh produce, and sells it in local markets. The business supplies vegetables and other farm products in both small quantities for individual customers and large quantities for traders and retailers. It is based in Lamin Village, The Gambia.

1. Fresh Vegetables

F.J Garden grows and sells fresh vegetables for households, restaurants, retailers, and market traders.

  • Tomatoes — estimated D100 per kg
  • Onions — estimated D80 per kg
  • Other seasonal vegetables — prices depend on availability

2. Root Crops

The farm grows root crops that customers can buy for household meals or resale in local markets.

  • Sweet potatoes — estimated D50 per kg
  • A Bag of sweet potatoes - estimated D6000 to D9500
  • Bulk sweet potatoes — price negotiable depending on quantity

3. Rice and Other Farm Produce

F.J Garden offers rice and other agricultural products when available from its farming activities or supply arrangements.

  • Rice — estimated D50 per kg
  • Other seasonal crops — contact the farm for availability

4. Wholesale and Retail Sales

Customers can purchase produce in small quantities for personal use or in larger quantities for shops, restaurants, and market resale.

  • Retail sales
    • Small quantities for households
    • Produce sold by the kilogram, Sam(wantel) or another agreed measure
  • Wholesale sales
    • Bulk quantities for traders and retailers
    • Bagged produce where suitable
    • Prices negotiated according to quantity and availability

5. How to Buy from F.J Garden

Customers can follow these steps to purchase farm produce.

  1. Contact F.J Garden to ask which crops are available.
  2. Choose the products and the quantity required.
  3. Confirm the price, measurement, and packaging.
  4. Arrange collection or delivery if available.
  5. Make payment according to the agreed terms.

Contact and Pricing Information

Prices may change according to the season, harvest size, market demand, and quantity purchased. Customers should contact F.J Garden for current prices, bulk orders, and available products.
